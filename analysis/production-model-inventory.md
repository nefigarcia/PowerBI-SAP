**Extraction method:** Direct read of TMDL (READ ONLY). This is the SOURCE OF TRUTH.

## model.tmdl

- **Culture:** en-GB
- **Source query culture:** en-IE
- **Compatibility level:** 1606
- **Default data source version:** powerBI_V3
- **Time intelligence:** enabled (`__PBI_TimeIntelligenceEnabled = 1`) — this is why there are ~40 auto-generated `LocalDateTable_*` tables (one per date column)
- **Data access options:** legacyRedirects, returnErrorValuesAsNull
- **Query order:** `SAP_SD_HL_BillingDocumentItem_V2`, `KNVV`, `User`, `Quote`, `Quote Line Item`, `Customer`, `SAP_SD_HL_SalesDocumentItem_V2`

## Auto date tables

There are ~44 `LocalDateTable_<guid>` tables plus 1 `DateTableTemplate_<guid>`. These are auto-generated by Power BI when Time Intelligence is enabled — one per date column across all business tables. They contain: Date, Year, MonthNo, Month, QuarterNo, Quarter, Day. **No custom DAX** — safe to skip when reverse-engineering, but the NEW project must decide whether to keep Time Intelligence on (produces the same auto-tables) or use a single shared Date table.

## Cultures

- `cultures/en-GB.tmdl` — declares the en-GB culture and Q&A linguistic metadata (generated bindings for entities/properties). Not part of business logic; can be regenerated by Power BI.
